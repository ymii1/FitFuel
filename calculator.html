How It Works

What do these numbers mean?

🔥

TDEE Key Metric

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure — the number of calories your body burns each day based on your stats and activity level.

⚖️

BMI

Body Mass Index is a simple ratio of weight to height. Under 18.5 = underweight, 18.5–24.9 = normal, 25–29.9 = overweight, 30+ = obese.

🧪

Macros Essential

Macronutrients — protein, carbohydrates, and fat — are the three main energy sources. Balancing them is key to body composition goals.
