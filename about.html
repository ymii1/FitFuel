Our Story

About FitFuel

A web-based meal tracking platform designed to help anyone eat smarter, feel better, and reach their goals.

FitFuel is a web-based meal tracking platform that helps users monitor their food intake, understand their nutrition, and reach their fitness goals. We believe that sustainable health isn't about restriction — it's about awareness. When you understand what you eat, you make better choices naturally.
🎯

Our Purpose New

To make nutrition tracking accessible, intuitive, and genuinely helpful — empowering people to build lasting healthy habits without complexity or overwhelm.

🌱

Our Approach

We combine simple tools — a meal logger, calorie calculator, and progress tracker — into one cohesive platform that fits naturally into your daily routine.

💡

Our Values Core

We believe in education over restriction, data over guesswork, and long-term lifestyle change over short-term diets. Health is personal — we give you the tools to define it.

Who is FitFuel for?

FitFuel is built for anyone who wants to take nutrition seriously — whether you're just starting out or optimizing a well-established routine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with FitFuel.

Yes — FitFuel is 100% free. All core features including meal logging, the nutrition calculator, and progress tracking are available at no cost. There are no hidden fees or premium tiers in this prototype.

All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage — it never leaves your device. As a prototype, we do not have a backend server or database. Your meals, weight logs, and account info stay entirely on your machine.

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — one of the most widely accepted formulas — to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). We then multiply it by your activity factor to get your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). Macro splits default to 30% protein, 40% carbs, and 30% fat.

Absolutely. FitFuel is built mobile-first and is fully responsive. Every page adapts seamlessly to phones, tablets, and desktops without any horizontal scrolling or layout breakage.

FitFuel is a student project built for a Web Development course. Planned features for future iterations include barcode scanning, cloud sync, social goal sharing, and recipe macro calculators. Stay tuned!
